The Navimow Creators Program is a long-term initiative that invites content creators to collaborate with Navimow on producing original video content related to robotic lawn mowers and smart gardening.
To participate, submit an application through the Navimow Creators Program application form. The form requires basic information such as your name, email, and social media profiles for evaluation.
Navimow evaluates applications based on creativity, production quality, audience engagement, and relevance to the Navimow brand image and garden-lifestyle themes.
Once approved, you will receive a welcome email from the Navimow team. All communications, including shipping arrangements and creative briefs, will be conducted through email.
Participants can receive:
  • Free Navimow products to test in real-life garden scenarios.
  • Real-time feedback on content from the Navimow team.
  • Increased exposure through reposts on Navimow's official social media platforms and email newsletters.
  • Affiliate commissions for qualified purchases made through your unique link.
  • Opportunities for paid collaborations and deeper long-term partnerships.
Yes, you must:
  • Be at least 18 years old.
  • Have an active social media account with a public profile.
  • Be willing to produce consistent, high-quality garden-related content.

Content must:
  • Be your original work.
  • Not involve purchased views.
  • Remain public for at least 90 days.
  • Be clearly labeled as sponsored.
